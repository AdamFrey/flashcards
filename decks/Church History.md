Q: Year of Pentecost?
A: c. AD 33

Q: Year the Jerusalem Temple was destroyed?
A: AD 70

Q: Year of the Edict of Milan?
A: 313

Q: What did the Edict of Milan do?
A: Granted religious tolerance to Christians

Q: Year of the First Council of Nicaea?
A: 325

Q: Ecumenical council in 325?
A: First Council of Nicaea

Q: What did Nicaea affirm?
A: Christ's full divinity (Nicene Creed)

Q: Year of the First Council of Constantinople?
A: 381

Q: What did Constantinople I affirm?
A: Divinity of the Holy Spirit

Q: Year of the Council of Ephesus?
A: 431

Q: What did Ephesus affirm?
A: Mary as Theotokos 

Q: Year of the Council of Chalcedon?
A: 451

Q: What did Chalcedon define?
A: Christ as one person, two natures

Q: Who defended Christ's divinity against Arianism?
A: Athanasius of Alexandria

Q: Who are the Cappadocian Fathers?
A: Basil the Great, Gregory of Nazianzus, Gregory of Nyssa

Q: What did the Cappadocian Fathers develop?
A: Trinitarian theology

Q: Who translated the Bible into Latin (the Vulgate)?
A: Jerome

Q: Who wrote Confessions and City of God?
A: Augustine of Hippo

Q: Who wrote Against Heresies (refuting Gnosticism)?
A: Irenaeus of Lyon

Q: Year of the Great Schism?
A: 1054

Q: Who posted the 95 Theses in 1517?
A: Martin Luther

Q: Year of the 95 Theses?
A: 1517

Q: Who wrote Institutes of the Christian Religion?
A: John Calvin
